Core Star Wars knowledge
------------------------
The Force:
- An energy field central to Star Wars mythology.
- Force-sensitive characters may learn to perceive or manipulate it.
- Star Wars often frames the Force through light-side and dark-side traditions, though individual
  stories complicate a simple binary.

Jedi:
- An order of Force-sensitive guardians associated with discipline, service and the light side.
- Jedi traditionally use lightsabers and undergo formal training.
- The Jedi Order is closely tied to the Galactic Republic in the prequel era.

Sith:
- A dark-side tradition centred on power and domination.
- In the film-era Sith lineage, the Rule of Two is an important organising principle: a master and
  an apprentice.
- Sith and Jedi are opposing traditions, but "dark-side user" and "Sith" are not automatically
  interchangeable labels.

Major political/factional context:
- Galactic Republic: the major democratic government before the Galactic Empire.
- Separatists / Confederacy of Independent Systems: opposition bloc in the Clone Wars.
- Galactic Empire: authoritarian regime established after the Republic's fall.
- Rebel Alliance: resistance movement opposing the Empire.
- New Republic: government established after the Empire's defeat.
- First Order and Resistance: major opposing factions in the sequel era.

Key characters commonly referenced:
- Yoda: Jedi Master known for wisdom, long life and distinctive speech.
- Obi-Wan Kenobi: Jedi mentor to Anakin Skywalker and later Luke Skywalker.
- Anakin Skywalker / Darth Vader: Jedi Knight who falls to the dark side and becomes Darth Vader.
- Luke Skywalker: central hero of the original trilogy and later Jedi figure.
- Leia Organa: political and military leader of the Rebellion/Resistance and member of the
  Skywalker family.
- Han Solo: smuggler who becomes a key Rebel ally.
- Emperor Palpatine / Darth Sidious: Sith Lord who engineers the fall of the Republic and becomes
  Emperor.
- Ahsoka Tano: former Jedi apprentice of Anakin Skywalker whose story spans multiple eras.
- Rey, Finn, Poe Dameron and Kylo Ren/Ben Solo are central figures in the sequel era.

Common concepts:
- Lightsaber: energy-blade weapon strongly associated with Jedi and Sith.
- Padawan: Jedi apprentice.
- Jedi Master: senior Jedi rank.
- Clone Wars: conflict between the Republic and Separatists preceding the Empire.
- Order 66: command used to turn clone forces against the Jedi near the end of the Clone Wars.
- Death Star: Imperial superweapon capable of destroying planets.
- Millennium Falcon: ship closely associated with Han Solo and Chewbacca.
- TIE fighter / X-wing: iconic Imperial and Rebel/Resistance starfighters.

Selected locations:
- Coruscant: Republic/Imperial capital world.
- Tatooine: desert world strongly connected to Anakin and Luke Skywalker.
- Alderaan: Leia Organa's homeworld.
- Hoth: icy Rebel-base setting in The Empire Strikes Back.
- Endor: site of the second Death Star conflict.
- Dagobah: where Yoda lives in exile and trains Luke.
- Mustafar: volcanic world associated with Anakin's fall and Darth Vader.
- Naboo: homeworld associated with Padmé Amidala and Palpatine.
